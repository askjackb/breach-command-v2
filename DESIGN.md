# Agreed design and implementation target

Realistic military science fiction, strong readable silhouettes, restrained palette, industrial mining colony versus subterranean organisms. StarCraft-inspired craft and readability; original designs. Primary target: iPhone 17, 30 FPS, mobile browser, portrait and landscape. Desktop also supported. Movable/rotatable/zoomable 3D camera.

Each run is independent. Procedurally generated terrain; no authored lanes or snapped tower placement. Signal warfare plus territory pressure: heat and vibration affect deterministic enemy target preferences; expansion creates valuable infrastructure. Towers physically occupy space but cannot prevent all attacks. Enemy classes reroute, attack obstacles or bypass them.

Endless waves with bosses. Destruction of the central command reactor ends the run. No pause or manual wave-start control. Mining and kills provide alloy, power capacity limits infrastructure. Run research starts from three tower types and progressively unlocks weapons and modifiers. Towers have three upgrade stages, two final specializations. Permanent unlocks broaden commander options without permanent damage bonuses. No active-run persistence.

Ten towers, ten enemy types, three bosses, three upgradeable active abilities, six interactive feature types. Free camera with bounded tilt; continuous placement requires confirmation. Partial sell refunds, construction time, paid repairs. Finite generated map with increasing territory; endless challenge after full expansion.

Quality acceptance remains an actual gameplay review: coherent material detail, distinct silhouettes, visible functional upgrade changes, convincing locomotion, readable weapon effects, and measured mobile performance together. The current build is the first implementation; see README for verification and known gaps.
