## Concept and atmosphere

An irregular subterranean opening large enough to make wave arrival credible. The environment must support the detailed military/biological art direction and the free camera.

## Production specification

Broken strata, a recessed dark throat, compressed dirt lip and restrained dust. The opening must have depth from a low-angle camera, not be a flat black circle. Keep anatomy cues subtle and avoid a fantasy portal. Show representative close detail and a normal battlefield view. Detail hierarchy must stay coherent with the reactor and towers; terrain should not compete with selectable units.

## Scale and delivery

approximately 5 m opening diameter, 1–2 m rim; terrain-relative root. Deliver editable source, required runtime files, channel/packing notes, bounds or tile scale, provenance and preview evidence under `public/assets/replacements/breach-mouth/`. Use the shared contract for file types, color spaces and metadata. If multiple modules belong to this kit, enumerate each module and its filename in the delivery report rather than presenting a single beauty image as the whole kit.

## Integration requirement

Current breach markers are circles at six spawn sites. New cavity geometry needs ground masking or careful rim placement to avoid a flat ground plane visibly sealing the hole. Current simulation rules remain authoritative. New decorative surface features cannot imply or secretly create fixed paths, build restrictions or resource mechanics.

## Generation instruction

Create a production-ready original realistic sci-fi mining-colony enemy breach opening using the physical forms and surface requirements above. Use restrained color and readable macrostructure. Deliver a usable asset package, not only a concept painting or a texture with baked lighting.

## Acceptance

Test continuity with neighboring surfaces, all-angle readability, visible tiling/repetition, alpha seams and texture density. Supply neutral-light and game-light previews. Reject generic primitive stand-ins, flat color, toy-like geology or effects that hide the battlefield.
