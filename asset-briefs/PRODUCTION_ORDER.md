# Production order and handoff

## Batch 1 — prove the look together

Base Autocannon, Skitter, reactor, basalt ground and basalt outcrop kit. The producing agent should show them in one representative actual 3D scene under both neutral and battlefield light, at normal gameplay distance and closest allowed zoom. Include cannon recoil/aim and Skitter locomotion/attack/death. Do not mass-produce the remaining roster before this batch establishes a reviewable direction.

This is a production quality gate, not permission to leave an assignment silently unfinished. Submit candidate assets with evidence and explicit missing work; the user or designated reviewer approves the style. If tasked with the whole set, explain dependencies and sequence rather than replacing unapproved assets with basic geometry.

## Batch 2 — distinguish weapon families and biological roles

Base Railgun, Missile, Cryo, Seismic and Drone hangar plus interceptor drone; Bastion, Razor, Burrower and Wasp; Mining rig and generator. Test comparison silhouettes and material consistency. Build the technical import/animation adapter with these varied shapes, not only one easy static asset.

## Batch 3 — complete base roster

Flak, Incinerator, Arc, Mortar; Climber, Artillery, Mender, Tracker and Brood carrier; Heat decoy and Seismic lure. Complete weapon presentation packages alongside their models. Establish appropriate emitter/muzzle/release sockets and short repeated audio variants.

## Batch 4 — progression and environmental interactions

All 40 tower upgrade/final-specialization variants, six map feature packages, shared terrain variations, construction/decal/debris sets. Review each variant beside its parent at equal camera and lighting. Keep footprints/pivots compatible. Ensure used feature presentation matches the game rather than implying a persistent effect.

## Batch 5 — bosses, commander presentation and interface

Three unique boss models/rigs, three commander effect packages, ability/tower/enemy/map-feature/resource icons, adaptive HUD references and title treatment. Ordinary enemy variants and bosses need their own animation evidence. Review boss scale with its escorts and a recognizable tower.

## Batch 6 — integrated polish and measured optimization

Complete audio/ambience/music, inspect all-angle consistency, choose LOD transitions from real-device profiling, measure worst-case effects and animated crowd cost, and verify portrait/landscape controls. Preserve editable high-quality sources when optimizing. Mark exactly which assets are delivered, reviewed and integrated; these are separate states.

## Handoff response format

For each batch provide: asset IDs; candidate version; delivery paths; preview links; model/texture/clip/source completeness; bounds/pivots/sockets; actual resource counts; tool/provenance; known defects; integration needs. A zip or beautiful image without ID/metadata is insufficient. The integrating agent can update the game incrementally once usable replacement assets are available.
